# The Great Reset Context

## Summary

The Great Reset is the transition narrative behind TIME Protocol.

It frames TIME as the human-anchored coordination layer required when dollar dominance, institutional legitimacy, information trust, and capital allocation begin to fracture.

## Relation to TIME Protocol

TIME Protocol gives executable form to The Great Reset through:

- proof of humanity;
- TIME as a human-hour unit;
- birthright allocation;
- work-backed minting;
- Work NFTs;
- Governance Agent;
- Shadow Democracy;
- Land Commons dividends;
- VolunteerPROOF;
- World Chain deployment;
- network-state and jurisdictional pilots.

## The Wallet Thesis

The Great Reset repo states that digital wallets are already powerful enough to challenge the dollar, but lack the coordination primitive underneath them.

TIME Protocol is that primitive.

The strongest formulation:

> Digital wallets created the rails. TIME supplies the human-anchored unit of account and coordination layer.

## Reset Date

The repo contains references to both 1/1/2030 and 1/1/2034.

Recommended handling:

```text
Use 1/1/2030 as the aspirational launch horizon.
Use 1/1/2034 as an extended transition horizon.
```

Do not hard-code one date everywhere until the public narrative is settled.

## Great Reset Modules Found in Repo

### TIME Protocol

The repo describes TIME Protocol as a global human time ledger where:

```text
1 TIME = 1 hour of verified human work
```

It also identifies World ID, blockchain infrastructure, and cross-chain interoperability as core infrastructure.

### Land Commons

Land Commons introduces a separate but complementary economic layer:

- land enters a shared commons;
- structures remain private property;
- land fees are collected;
- dividends are distributed equally to verified humans.

This should remain separate from TIME token issuance.

### VolunteerPROOF

VolunteerPROOF verifies volunteer hours and mints proof NFTs, with future TIME integration.

It is a direct reference app for unpaid or public-good contribution.

### World Chain Deployment

The repo includes deployed World Chain contract addresses for HumanBond, TIME Token, VowNFT, and MilestoneNFT.

These should be tracked in a dedicated deployment file.

## Narrative Role

The Great Reset is not the protocol spec.

It is the civilizational story:

> The old coordination systems are failing. TIME is the human-time replacement layer.
