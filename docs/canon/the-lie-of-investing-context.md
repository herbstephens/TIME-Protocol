# The Lie of Investing Context

## Summary

The Lie of Investing should be incorporated as the capital-allocation diagnosis behind TIME Protocol.

It explains why TIME emphasizes work, local allocation, public goods, and human contribution over abstract financial accumulation.

## Core Problem

Modern investing often separates ownership from responsibility.

Capital flows into:

- index funds;
- distant corporations;
- opaque financial products;
- speculative assets;
- debt structures;
- extractive platforms;
- systems the investor does not understand and cannot influence.

This creates a false sense of participation.

The investor thinks they are building wealth, but may be funding extraction, political capture, or systems that undermine their own community.

## TIME Protocol Connection

TIME Protocol reframes allocation around human time.

Instead of asking:

> What asset will appreciate?

TIME asks:

> Whose time, work, place, and institution should receive support?

## Local Allocation

Local allocation is the protocol answer to abstract investing.

A user should be able to allocate TIME to:

- local businesses;
- neighbors;
- cooperatives;
- public goods;
- volunteer work;
- civic projects;
- officials;
- laws;
- budgets;
- network states;
- alternative institutions.

## Work-Backed Money vs Capital-Backed Claims

Traditional investing begins with capital.

TIME begins with human work.

The key inversion:

```text
Capital is not the base layer.
Human time is the base layer.
```

## Implications

The Lie of Investing context supports these TIME design choices:

- work-backed minting;
- Work NFTs;
- local opportunity feeds;
- Governance Agent;
- public-goods allocation;
- human-to-human fee-free transfers;
- corporate/capital commons fees;
- Land Commons dividends;
- anti-speculative framing.

## Recommended Use in TIME Repo

Use this context to explain why TIME should not be marketed primarily as a speculative token.

TIME should be framed as:

- a unit of human work;
- a local allocation instrument;
- a governance signal;
- a public-goods coordination layer;
- a wage-economy settlement asset.

## One-Line Summary

> The Lie of Investing critiques abstract capital. TIME Protocol replaces it with human-time allocation.
