# The Book Trilogy and TIME Protocol

## Overview

The three books form a narrative ladder leading to TIME Protocol.

```text
1. The Lie Factory
2. The Lie of Investing
3. The Great Reset
4. TIME Protocol
```

The first two books diagnose the failure modes of the current system. The third describes the transition. TIME Protocol provides the executable infrastructure.

## Book 1: The Lie Factory

**Core role:** information crisis.

The Lie Factory should be treated as the diagnosis of manufactured reality:

- manipulated public narratives;
- captured media;
- distorted consensus;
- institutional propaganda;
- political identity manufacturing;
- algorithmic information control;
- synthetic legitimacy.

TIME Protocol relevance:

- governance requires truthful signals;
- Shadow Democracy needs verified humans, not bot swarms;
- Governance Agent helps individuals see who governs them;
- proof of humanity becomes a defense against synthetic political reality.

## Book 2: The Lie of Investing

**Core role:** capital-allocation crisis.

The Lie of Investing should be treated as the critique of abstract investing:

- capital detached from place;
- ownership without responsibility;
- passive indexing as political surrender;
- financialization of everything;
- extraction disguised as participation;
- retirement systems that force people to fund systems they oppose.

TIME Protocol relevance:

- local allocation replaces abstract investing;
- users allocate TIME toward people, places, businesses, laws, and institutions they understand;
- work-backed issuance replaces passive financial claims;
- capital becomes subordinate to human time.

## Book 3: The Great Reset

**Core role:** transition thesis.

The Great Reset should be treated as the historical and political moment when existing monetary, information, investment, governance, and identity systems become unstable enough for replacement infrastructure to matter.

TIME Protocol relevance:

- TIME becomes the human-anchored coordination layer;
- digital wallets become the distribution surface;
- proof of humanity becomes the population layer;
- work receipts become the economic history layer;
- Governance Agent becomes the political awareness layer;
- Land Commons and VolunteerPROOF become implementation modules.

## Protocol Relationship

The books should not be quoted excessively inside the protocol repo.

Instead, they should be referenced as canon:

```text
The Lie Factory explains why governance signals are corrupted.
The Lie of Investing explains why capital allocation is corrupted.
The Great Reset explains why a transition layer is necessary.
TIME Protocol is that transition layer.
```

## Recommended Public Narrative

> The trilogy diagnoses three failures: information, investment, and institutional coordination. TIME Protocol is the constructive answer: a human-time-denominated monetary and governance layer for the next economy.

## Recommended Repository Linkage

Each book should live in its own repo or publishing channel.

TIME Protocol should include:

- short summaries;
- relationship to protocol primitives;
- links to book repositories or websites;
- extracted concepts that become technical requirements.

Do not place full manuscripts in the TIME Protocol repo unless the goal is explicitly to make TIME Protocol a book-and-code monorepo.
