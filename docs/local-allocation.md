# Local Allocation

## Thesis

Investment has become abstract.

People allocate capital to companies they will never visit, funds they do not understand, governments they cannot influence, and markets detached from their own daily lives.

TIME Protocol reframes investment as allocation.

> Allocate to what you know, who you know, and where you live.

## Local-First Principle

A human should be able to allocate TIME, money, energy, reputation, and attention to:

- local businesses;
- neighbors;
- cooperatives;
- mutual aid projects;
- public goods;
- local infrastructure;
- schools;
- care networks;
- community solar;
- food systems;
- housing projects;
- civic institutions;
- political alternatives.

## Governance Agent Role

The Governance Agent discovers local opportunities and explains their governance context.

Example:

```text
You want to open a bakery.

The agent shows:
- which permits are required;
- which officials approve them;
- which zoning laws apply;
- which council vote is upcoming;
- which local suppliers are nearby;
- which community funds could support you;
- which TIME allocations would help.
```

## Allocation vs Speculation

Speculation asks:

> What number will go up?

Allocation asks:

> What human, place, or institution should receive my scarce time and support?

TIME Protocol should privilege allocation over speculation.

## Local Opportunity Feed

A reference implementation should include:

- local businesses seeking support;
- verified work opportunities;
- cooperatives seeking members;
- local public-goods funds;
- volunteer needs;
- neighborhood projects;
- local governance proposals;
- jurisdiction-specific budget needs.

## TIME Signals

Allocating TIME locally creates multiple signals:

| Signal | Meaning |
|---|---|
| TIME staked to business | trust / support / future patronage |
| TIME staked to official | political confidence |
| TIME moved away from official | desire for replacement |
| TIME staked to public good | participatory budgeting preference |
| TIME earned locally | proof of contribution to place |
| TIME spent locally | local economic circulation |

## Local Economy Loop

```text
Verify humanity
    ↓
Claim birthright TIME
    ↓
Discover local governance and opportunities
    ↓
Work locally / support locally
    ↓
Earn TIME and reputation
    ↓
Stake TIME to local governance and public goods
    ↓
Build parallel local economy
```

## Product Principle

Make the local world visible.

The app should help users see:

- who governs their life;
- where their money goes;
- who near them needs help;
- which institutions are extractive;
- which local alternatives are viable.
