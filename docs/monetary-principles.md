# Monetary Principles of TIME Protocol

## Principle 1: The Hour Is More Fundamental Than the Dollar

The dollar is a sovereign unit.  
The hour is a human unit.

Dollars are issued by a state.  
Hours are issued by life.

TIME Protocol begins from the premise that human time is a more universal monetary reference than any national currency.

---

## Principle 2: Scarcity Must Be Real

Fiat scarcity is political.  
Bitcoin scarcity is mathematical.  
Gold scarcity is geological.  
TIME scarcity is biological.

A verified human has only 24 hours per day.

This is the core scarcity constraint of TIME Protocol.

---

## Principle 3: Work Must Be Verified

A monetary system based on human time must prevent false claims.

TIME therefore requires verification of:

- human identity;
- time allocation;
- contractual commitment;
- work completion or satisfaction;
- payment.

Without verification, time tokens degrade into unbacked labor claims.

---

## Principle 4: Payment Creates Economic Backing

TIME is strongest when minted only after real payment is received.

This transforms TIME from a mere record of activity into a payment-backed monetary unit.

The economic backing is not theoretical. It is demonstrated by a counterparty willing to pay for the verified human hour.

---

## Principle 5: No Double-Booking

The same verified human hour cannot be sold twice.

This principle is essential to maintaining the integrity of the supply.

A valid TIME Protocol must include calendar, scheduling, escrow, or proof-of-time mechanisms that prevent overlapping claims.

---

## Principle 6: Stability Requires a Reference Unit

No asset is stable in the abstract.

Every stability claim must answer:

> Stable relative to what?

TIME is stable relative to verified human hours.

It should not claim absolute purchasing-power stability or a fixed dollar price unless a separate mechanism is introduced.

---

## Principle 7: Human Time Should Be Programmable

TIME should support smart contracts for:

- work agreements;
- escrow;
- payroll;
- streaming payments;
- recurring work;
- UBI;
- tax routing;
- reputation;
- dispute resolution;
- credentials;
- DAO governance;
- public goods funding.

The goal is not simply to represent hours, but to make human economic coordination programmable.

---

## Principle 8: The Protocol Should Be Globally Neutral

TIME should not depend on the monetary policy of any one nation.

It may trade against dollars, euros, bitcoin, ether, and other assets. But its native unit should remain the verified human hour.

---

## Principle 9: UBI Is a Liquidity Layer

A baseline TIME-denominated UBI can be understood as liquidity for the human economy.

Just as blockchains use token issuance or airdrops to bootstrap networks, societies may use UBI to maintain circulation, participation, and demand.

TIME makes this logic programmable and globally interoperable.

---

## Principle 10: The AI Economy Needs Proof of Humanity

As AI-generated output becomes abundant, verified human work becomes more important.

TIME can serve as a protocol for distinguishing human participation from synthetic production.

In an AI-saturated world, proof of humanity and proof of human time become foundational economic primitives.
