**TIME Protocol: Governance Agent on Soroban**

## What We're Building

A quadratic-voting, multi-jurisdictional governance system on Soroban enabling verified humans to allocate their TIME holdings — including a retroactive birthright grant proportional to age — across the Governance Objects that govern their lives: laws, budgets, officials, and public goods.

---

## Why Soroban

Three reasons that make Soroban architecturally required (not a superficial integration):

1. **Fee accessibility** — governance actions must be economically viable for a verified human in Lagos as much as Lisbon. Ethereum L1 excludes this use case.
2. **Cross-border settlement** — the JurisdictionRegistry handles TIME allocation across multiple currency and legal jurisdictions via Stellar's path payment system.
3. **Regulatory posture** — engagement with governments, NGOs, and international organizations requires Stellar's compliance-friendly reputation.
