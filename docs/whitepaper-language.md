## Abstract Draft

Today’s dollar stablecoins are misnamed. They are not stable in any universal economic sense; they are merely pegged to the U.S. dollar. For users outside the dollar economy, they import exchange-rate volatility, monetary dependence, and exposure to U.S. policy. They stabilize a token against a fiat unit whose own purchasing power fluctuates continuously against labor, goods, services, and other currencies.

TIME Protocol proposes a different reference asset: verified human time. One TIME represents one verified hour of human productive capacity, minted only when real work is contracted, verified, and paid for. Unlike fiat-backed stablecoins, TIME is not stabilized by reserves of government debt. It is stabilized by the biological scarcity of human life, the cryptographic uniqueness of verified persons, and market-clearing payments for human work.

Fiat stablecoins tokenize yesterday’s money. TIME tokenizes the fundamental input of tomorrow’s economy: human time.

---

## Problem Statement

The dominant stablecoin model is built around fiat currency pegs, especially the U.S. dollar. While these instruments are useful for payments, trading, and settlement, they fail to provide universal stability.

A dollar-pegged stablecoin is stable only relative to the dollar. It remains volatile against other currencies, against real purchasing power, and against the lived economic reality of most of the world’s population.

This creates a category error. The market calls these assets stablecoins, but most are more accurately described as tokenized dollar instruments.

TIME Protocol begins with a different question:

> What if the reference unit for digital money were not a sovereign currency, but verified human time?

---

## Core Claim

TIME Protocol is a human-time stablecoin protocol.

Its native unit, TIME, represents one verified hour of human productive capacity. TIME is designed to be minted only when work is performed by a verified human and paid for by a real counterparty.

This makes TIME a work-backed, payment-settled, human-time-denominated monetary asset.

---

## USD Stablecoin Critique

USD stablecoins are powerful but limited.

They make dollars programmable, but they do not make value stable. They inherit the dollar’s inflation, foreign-exchange volatility, monetary policy exposure, banking dependencies, and geopolitical asymmetries.

For a user in a dollar economy, a dollar stablecoin may feel stable. For a user outside that economy, it is a floating foreign-currency asset.

The issue is not that USD stablecoins are useless. The issue is that their stability claim is overstated.

They are stable against a currency, not against human economic reality.

---

## TIME Alternative

TIME uses the verified human hour as its reference unit.

Every person receives the same daily endowment of time. No central bank can print more hours. No government can inflate the length of a day. No corporation can manufacture additional human life.

By anchoring issuance to verified humans, non-overlapping time commitments, completed work, and real payment, TIME creates a monetary asset based on productive human contribution rather than fiat reserves.

## Monetary Framing

Bitcoin’s scarcity is mathematical.  
Gold’s scarcity is geological.  
Fiat’s scarcity is political.  
TIME’s scarcity is biological.

This biological scarcity gives TIME its foundational monetary property.

A dollar can be devalued.  
An hour cannot be inflated.

---

## Work-Backed Issuance

TIME is not backed by labor in the abstract.

It is backed by market-clearing payment for verified human work.

The distinction is crucial. A person claiming to have worked does not by itself create monetary value. But when another party pays for a verified hour of human work, the protocol can record that event and mint TIME as a settlement asset.

This connects token issuance to real economic demand.

---

## Human Proof-of-Work

Bitcoin introduced machine proof-of-work.

TIME introduces human proof-of-work.

Unlike machine mining, human proof-of-work is not wasted computation. It is useful contribution: care, service, creativity, coordination, judgment, labor, and responsibility.

TIME converts verified human contribution into programmable money.

## Government / Public Policy Narrative

A global digital economy requires a neutral unit for human participation.

Dollar stablecoins may increase dollarization and monetary dependency. TIME offers an alternative: a non-sovereign, human-centered unit of account that can support work verification, public goods funding, UBI, and transparent tax routing.

Because TIME is based on verified human hours rather than national currency issuance, it can serve as a complementary economic layer for governments, communities, and international institutions.
